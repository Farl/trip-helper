Build a pack of concrete experiences that everyone can answer independently. The repository schemas are the single source of truth: read [shared/types.ts](../../shared/types.ts), [shared/localization.ts](../../shared/localization.ts), the content validator, and the relevant existing pack before writing content.

## Research a pack

Discuss destination, dates/year, party age range, trip style, and relevant restrictions early. Use already supplied answers; where a nonessential detail is missing, state a planning assumption and continue. Age guides variety; it must not prefilter cards or imply that all participants have the same ability or taste. Present the proposed breadth when the user has not already set it.

Choose experiences at the level people can meaningfully distinguish: looking at a building, entering a museum, buying a ticket for a view, trying a specific food, browsing shops, walking or resting. Several cards may share a stable `placeId`; interest in one must not answer another. `category` and `tags` support later analysis only. Every participant sees the same version and the same cards. Record only `interested` / `not_interested`; no answer stays unanswered.

Use official government, venue, park, transit, and restaurant sources for current operations and concrete facts. Firsthand travel blogs and vlogs are also useful sources of taste, atmosphere, queues and activity details; attribute the creator and publication or visit date, retain their source link, and distinguish subjective observations from guarantees for all visitors. Cross-check dates, fees, closures and opening conditions against official sources. Verify source contents and attach `checkedAt`. For Taipei use the city tourism API with `Accept: application/json`; when blocked, the configured daily Tourism Administration dataset is a primary-source fallback. This repository's reusable collector is [scripts/collect-taipei.ts](../../scripts/collect-taipei.ts), with endpoints, trip scope, and original card proposals in [taipei-config.json](../../public/trips/sources/taipei-config.json). Change config for content, rather than adding per-card code.

Use media that reveals the specific experience's appeal and practical tradeoffs. Food cards need actual food, hands-on activities need the activity or setup, and architecture cards can show the particular structure being examined. A street sign, generic landmark or venue exterior cannot substitute for a dish, exhibit or activity. Open and visually inspect the original photo, not just its filename, caption or alt text. Preserve its URL, accurate alt text, photographer/agency credit and source URL. Do not use posters with historical price/date text that could be read as current during a quick swipe.

Record a stable `mediaIdentity` based on the original asset ID plus visual comparison, including alternative sizes, crops and URL aliases. Repeated media or similar experiences may be intentional independent opportunities to react; keep distinct card IDs and explain the repetition in `mediaReview`. Repetition is a diagnostic, not an automatic reason to delete useful content. Use images for naturally visual experiences such as food, museum objects, interactive exhibits, baths and shops. A full-text card must stand on its own: a concrete, attractive proposal whose pace, method or tradeoff is enough to decide yes/no. Record a positive `textOnlyReason` explaining that editorial choice, not a missing-photo, rights or unpublished-event problem. If suitable media remains unavailable and the text cannot support a meaningful decision, replace or remove the experience rather than filling a card quota. Do not invent games or unusual activities just to justify text; clearly label self-directed proposals and never attribute them to a venue. Do not fill gaps with generated images, stock images or unrelated venue photos. Media quantity is secondary to fidelity.

For video, confirm the real playable source and reuse rights. Make short clips only when the original can legally be obtained and reused, and record attribution and clip boundaries; otherwise use a verified source link or text. Keep published remote media at its source when reuse rights have not been established for local redistribution. Temporary downloads may support authorized visual inspection, not evade display restrictions. Record source/rights basis, rejected images and unresolved conditions per card in a research audit; do not copy source articles.

Write concise original Traditional Chinese descriptions and a complete, natural English presentation of the same experiences. State planning estimates as estimates in both languages. Do not fabricate future events, menu prices, opening hours, exhibit availability or reservations. Prefer `現場／官方公告為準` / `Confirm on site or with official announcements` for unresolved costs. Describe known stairs, slopes, standing, crowds or conditions concretely; do not claim universal accessibility. Mark weather, seasonal displays and unpublished event schedules in `facts.seasonalNote`, or omit the uncertain experience. Translate every caveat with the same strength; English must not turn a proposal, estimate, or unknown into a confirmed fact.

The exact card fields are `id`, `placeId`, `title`, `description`, `category`, `tags`, optional `image` / `video`, `source`, and `facts`; use the linked TypeScript interfaces for nested field shapes. The pack includes `id`, `version`, `title`, `destination`, `startsOn`, `endsOn`, `audience`, `intro`, and `cards`. Keep [public/trips/index.json](../../public/trips/index.json) summaries consistent with the real pack.

Traditional Chinese is canonical. Store English separately at `public/trips/locales/en/{tripId}/{tripVersion}.json`, following `TripTranslation` and `translationPath`. Include all card IDs and every display field, including image alt text, original photo attribution, source titles and facts. Preserve photographer names. Both languages use the same canonical `tripId`, `tripVersion`, card/place IDs, dates, sources, media and binary choices. Switching language must only change presentation; it must not create another pack, filter experiences, change votes or regenerate existing invitation versions. Translation edits that preserve meaning do not modify the canonical pack or registry.

## Refresh and validate

Run `node --import tsx scripts/collect-taipei.ts` from the repository root; `--config PATH` selects config. Every plan explicitly selects `image` plus `mediaIdentity`, or `textOnly` plus a positive `textOnlyReason`, with `mediaReview`, complete `source` and `cost`; put cross-checks in `supportingSources`. Store each source's actual manual verification date in `Plan.source.checkedAt`; update it only after rechecking that source. The collector preserves those dates and validates the generated trip with the runtime schema before any content write. Fetching the government catalog does not reverify external articles, brand menus, blogs or venue pages.

The collector never substitutes a catalog's first venue photo and reports duplicate underlying identities and size-normalized URLs for review. For an already verified official archive extracted locally, use `--input PATH_TO_AttractionList.json --checked-at YYYY-MM-DD`. This CLI date records the dataset fetch/inspection in `manifest.checkedAt` only; it never replaces card-source or supporting-source review dates. A timestamp records an actual verification, not an assumed freshness; use the configured trip time zone rather than UTC. API source name changes intentionally fail collection so a human-readable review can resolve them.

Use `--draft PATH` to write a reviewed candidate without touching published files. Produce its complete English sidecar against the draft's semantic version before publication. The collector retains the previous canonical pack under `public/trips/sources/archives/{tripId}/{tripVersion}.json` when publishing a new version, and leaves old English sidecars intact. Preserve the published order and reasonable card/place IDs when the user requested a media/content refinement, so each participant still sees the same order. A materially narrowed or changed experience gets a new card ID so analytics do not merge old general preferences with a new specific brand, dish or activity.

The collector hashes scope and card meaning into `version`, excluding verification timestamps. Confirm an identical input produces identical content/version. It checks English against that generated version before publishing, preserves all locale files, and stops with a report when English is missing, invalid or incomplete. Create the reviewed English copy for a new version before publishing; do not auto-translate source refreshes with unverified additions. Before changing a live pack's meaning or card IDs, inspect vote/version behavior and preserve the old canonical and English versions needed by existing participants; do not reinterpret old votes against edited cards. Refresh provenance dates without inventing content changes. Run `npm run validate:content` for full two-language coverage, then inspect representative cards and verify media responses. Report unresolved facts and date assumptions alongside the delivered pack.

## Analyze exported choices

Read [analysis guidance](references/analysis.md) when using raw JSON/CSV exports. Preserve direct answers and source evidence, distinguish unanswered from negative, and propose a reviewable itinerary with tradeoffs. Preference analysis is not a psychological or demographic classification.
